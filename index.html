Our story

From fire station to family practice.

Wes grew up on Evansville's west side. Jessica came from Logansport. They met in dental school, came home, and gave an old firehouse a second life.

  1. 2006

    Dr. Wes Brown graduates from IU School of Dentistry and opens his practice back home.

  2. 2007

    Dr. Jessica Brown joins her husband after a year-long associateship with Evansville dentists.

  3. 2011

    They buy the historic Hose House No. 5 on St. Joe Ave and spend six months restoring it to its former glory.

  4. Today

    A unique, welcoming office caring for children, adults and seniors across Evansville.
